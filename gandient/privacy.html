Gandient Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Gandient does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data. All gradient projects, meshes, effects, and motion loops are processed locally on your device.

When you choose to save an exported image or video to your Photos library, Gandient requests add-only Photos access solely to write your artwork. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers are used.

Contact: gannonphotos.com
